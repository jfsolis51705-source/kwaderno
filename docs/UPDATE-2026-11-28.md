## 1. Data the owner controls (three sharing levels)

Store owners choose what KELSON receives. They choose at sign-up and can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & my data.

| Level | What is shared | What it powers | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Always | Store name and area, number of products and low-stock items, questions to Kwaderno | Running the network | Privacy Notice |
| 1 · Help KWADERNO learn | Products, stock counts, units sold, barcodes, shelf photos. No money | A smarter LVM | Opt-in |
| 2 · Sales summary | Daily sales totals, payment mix, stock value, total utang. No receipts, no customer names | Market reports, tips | Opt-in |
| 3 · Apply for credit | Full sales history, only to a named lender | Credit score for a quick loan | Asked again for each application |

- Customer names and each single sale never leave the phone.
- Exports leave out any area with fewer than 5 stores.
- The Privacy Notice (version 2026-11-28.2) says KWADERNO does not give data to the BIR or other stores and does not sell it, except when the law requires.
- Level 3 only records the application. Nothing goes to a lender until a licensed lending partner is signed and the owner confirms again.
- Stores that agreed before the levels existed keep sending totals until they review the new notice. After that, sales sharing starts unticked.
- In Super Admin, the compliance card counts the stores at each level. A store that keeps its money private shows "Not shared", and its sales chart is hidden.

## 2. The three engines now help store owners

The new "Check my shelf (offline)" card is on the store's Shelf photos screen. The owner photographs a shelf, and the phone does the rest:
- ♍ Virgo finds the packs and names them.
- ♒ Aquarius's trained network helps name them.
- ♎ Libra compares the shelf with the app's stock.

The result shows:
- **Restock:** products low in the app, plus what the photo saw.
- **Refill the shelf:** items with stock in the app but only one pack showing.
- **Count doesn't match:** the shelf shows it but the app says 0, or the shelf shows far more than the app.

It runs offline with no AI, and the photo stays on the phone.

How stores get it:
- Super Admin sends an "engine pack" after each Coach run or training, or with the "📤 Send to stores" button.
- The pack holds product fingerprints (numbers, not photos), the trained network and Libra's best setting.
- Store photos are only used when the store opted in.

Limit: pack counts come from edges in the photo, so striped packaging can be over-counted. For this reason Libra flags only clear mismatches. Test on real shelves before relying on counts.

## 3. Backend (the hive's database)

The Supabase project in Singapore holds:
- store accounts, staff and branches;
- the level-filtered summary from each store;
- credit applications;
- guidance sent down to stores (engine pack, rules, tips);
- an area report for Super Admin.

The server enforces the rules, whatever an app sends:
- Anything a store did not agree to share is dropped.
- Money is removed from level 1. Customer names are removed from level 2.
- Turning a level off deletes what was already shared at that level.
- A deletion request stops all syncing.
- Credit applications can only be "waiting" or "withdrawn".
- The area report works only for Super Admin, and only for cities with 5 or more stores.

Tested (inside a transaction, then undone):
- An owner sees only their own store.
- Another user sees nothing and can't write.
- Super Admin sees all and can send guidance.
- The level filters and the 5-store rule held.

## 5. Weekly Philippine legal check

It runs every Monday at 9:43 AM Manila time and writes to `docs/legal/PH-COMPLIANCE.md` on the `legal-watch` branch. It now also watches the lending and credit laws:

| Law | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| RA 9510, Credit Information System Act | Credit scoring with alternative data |
| RA 9474, Lending Company Regulation Act | Lenders must be SEC-licensed; rules for online lending |
| RA 8556, Financing Company Act | Financing companies |
| RA 11765, Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act | Fair treatment and disclosure for borrowers |
| RA 3765, Truth in Lending Act | Full disclosure of loan cost |

New open actions:
- A lawyer reviews the three levels and the credit consent.
- Before the credit pilot: an SEC-licensed lending partner, a data-sharing agreement, the RA 9510 position, and loan disclosures.

Not legal advice: a Philippine lawyer should confirm before launch.
