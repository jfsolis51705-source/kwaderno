How one day flows

Sign in

Password, then fingerprint on the phone.

Sell & count

Stores sell, scan and clock in, offline.

Share

Only the chosen level goes to the main server.

Engines learn

Virgo reads, Libra checks, Aquarius trains.

Report

Results go to Super Admin and the team chat.

Guide

New pack, photos and tips go back to the stores.
