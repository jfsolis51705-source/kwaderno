Hard · read when something goes wrong

Fix problems

Most problems are fixed in 3 tries: 1) refresh the page, 2) check your internet, 3) sign out and in again. If it still fails, ask Claude with a ready message below.

What you seeTry this first
No Super Admin buttonYou are signed in with another account. Sign out of claude.ai and sign in as the owner.
A page is empty or spinningRefresh. Wait 10 seconds. Check internet.
Store shows "Not shared"Not a problem: the owner keeps money private (Level 2 off).
Product photo missing📷 Photo library → add the photo. Stores get it automatically.
"Check my shelf" button is greySuper Admin hasn't sent the engine pack. Do Level 7, step 5.
Training stoppedKeep the screen on and the page open. Use a computer if the phone is slow. Try again.
Scanner won't open the cameraOpen the scanner in Chrome or Safari (not inside Facebook/Messenger) and allow the camera.
"Could not save"Check internet. Make sure you are signed in as the owner.

Ask Claude to fix it (copy, then paste in your Claude chat)

KWADERNO problem: [what you see]. It happens when I [what you tapped]. Device: [phone / tablet / computer]. Screenshot attached. Please fix it and tell me when it's live.
Please run a full KWADERNO health check: app, Super Admin, scanner, backend security, legal check, and the engines. Fix anything broken and send me a short report.
Please undo the last KWADERNO change. Something stopped working after it.
SafeEvery change Claude makes is saved in GitHub, so an older working version can always be brought back.
